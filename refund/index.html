Refund & Cancellation Policy

Clear terms about how refunds and cancellations are handled for D&L Project56 services.

General Policy

D&L Project56 provides travel planning and coordination services. Refunds and cancellations depend on the nature of the service and third-party provider involvement.

Service-Based Cancellations

Cancellation by the client: If you cancel our travel planning service before work begins, a full refund may be available. Once we have started planning your trip and communicating with providers, refunds depend on work completed and any costs incurred.

Cancellation by D&L Project56: We reserve the right to cancel if you do not respond to communications within a reasonable timeframe or if service cannot be properly delivered.

Third-Party Bookings

For accommodations, transfers, and other services booked through third-party providers:

  • The provider's cancellation policy applies, not D&L Project56's
  • Read the provider's terms before confirming any booking
  • Non-refundable rates are non-refundable by the provider and by us
  • We cannot override a provider's cancellation policy
  • Refunds from providers are processed directly between you and the provider

Changes Requested by You

If you request changes to bookings (different dates, different accommodation, etc.):

  • Changes are subject to the provider's modification policy
  • Fees or penalties may apply depending on timing
  • We will help coordinate changes but cannot guarantee availability or cost
  • Any additional costs are your responsibility

Non-Refundable Situations

No refund is available for:

  • Services already delivered (planning completed, advice provided)
  • Third-party cancellation penalties (when you cancel with the provider)
  • Changes made by third parties beyond our control
  • Your failure to appear or use booked services

Case-by-Case Resolution

Unusual situations (provider bankruptcy, service failure, extraordinary circumstances) are handled on a case-by-case basis. Contact us immediately if something unexpected occurs during your planned trip.

We will work with you to find reasonable solutions within the limits of what third-party providers allow.
