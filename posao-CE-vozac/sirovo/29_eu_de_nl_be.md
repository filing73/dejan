Notes:
- Germany permit route: Serbia falls under the Westbalkanregelung (§ 26 Abs. 2 BeschV, quota 50,000/yr, needs concrete job offer, BA approval before visa). It opens the labour market only. It does NOT replace licence or Code 95. Route §24a Abs. 2 BeschV (qualify in Germany within 15 months, employer bears course contract) requires German B1 as entry condition. Dejan has no German, so it is not realistic now. Source: https://www.ipwiki.de/bundesrecht:beschv:berufskraftfahrerinnen_und_berufskraftfahrer ; https://www.talentorbit.de/arbeitgeber/lkw-fahrer-aus-dem-ausland/
- Code 95: a Serbian Basisqualifikation is generally not recognised in Germany; a Slovenian (EU) Code 95 was valid in the EU but has expired, so he would need to redo the Grundqualifikation, and exams are mostly German-only (VerkehrsRundschau). Employers carry the legal liability for the driver's right to drive since Nov 2023, so careful ones avoid beginners from third countries.
- Netherlands: employer needs TWV/GVVA (UWV/IND) after a 5-week vacancy test, plus a NIWO Bestuurdersattest (driver must live in NL, hold C with Code 95, employer holds Eurovergunning). Practically hard for a beginner. Source: https://www.niwo.nl/nl/producten/overige-documenten/bestuurdersattest-goederenvervoer ; https://ondernemersplein.overheid.nl/wetten-en-regels/tewerkstellingsvergunning-twv-voor-buitenlandse-werknemers/
- Belgium: single permit (permis unique/eenheidsvergunning) applied for by the employer; Code 95 mandatory; pay about EUR 16-22/h gross; no sponsor found.
- Red flags: Jan 2026 EES border enforcement led to expulsions of Balkan drivers (https://de.euronews.com/my-europe/2026/01/27/protest-eu-lkw-fahrer-ees); NL Arbeidsinspectie found 8 illegally employed drivers in May 2026. Avoid anyone offering work without a proper permit or asking for a fee.
- Realistic picture: for a 53-year-old with no CE experience, no German and an expired Code 95, none of DE/NL/BE offers a viable direct-hire path; Poland/Slovenia/Croatia/Baltics-type countries are likelier.
