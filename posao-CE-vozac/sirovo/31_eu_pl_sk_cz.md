## Notes (realistic picture)
- Poland: Serbians are NOT in the simplified "oswiadczenie" list (UA, BY, GE, MD, AM, RU), so the route is a work permit (zezwolenie na prace / single permit) applied for by the employer at the voivode (~1-4 months). Carrier also needs "swiadectwo kierowcy" (GITD) for each non-EU driver on international haulage. Code 95 initial qualification can be done in Poland (~3,100 PLN course, courses exist in English/Russian). Most Polish carriers ask 1 yr CE experience; no-experience offers are mostly domestic C/CE routes, not sponsoring non-EU drivers. Pay figures NP.
- Slovakia: international HGV driver is a shortage profession, so a simplified procedure without labour-market test applies (employer gets confirmation of vacancy, driver applies for temporary residence; may start work before decision; Serbia visa-free). Older rules gave Serbia and BiH a national-visa quota for drivers (dated, verify). Employer must also obtain "osvedcenie vodica" for non-EU drivers. Sources: https://www.akmv.sk/ako-zamestnat-cudzincov-vodica-kamionu-krok-za-krokom/ , https://employment.gov.sk/sk/uvodna-stranka/informacie-media/aktuality/cudzinci-mozu-doplnit-chybajucu-pracovnu-silu-doprave-jednoduchsie.html . Legally the most promising country, but no named beginner-friendly employer found.
- Czech Republic: Serbia is in the "Kvalifikovany zamestnanec" programme (employee card with annual quotas, applied by the driver at the Czech embassy in Belgrade, 60-90 days); needs Serbian criminal record extract. Many ads accept beginners ("zaucime") but none confirmed to hire Serbians. https://mpo.gov.cz/cz/zahranicni-obchod/ekonomicka-migrace/program-kvalifikovany-zamestnanec--248247/
- Red flags: many "visa sponsorship" pages are agencies charging fees; Code 95/CPC and driver attestation are always needed; beginner + non-EU + permit sponsorship is a rare combination everywhere. Suggested next step: write directly to Slovak/Czech carriers from job boards (sluzbyzamestnanosti.gov.sk, truckjobs.cz) asking explicitly about the simplified procedure / employee card for a Serbian CE holder.
