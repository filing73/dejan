## Permit route and realistic picture for Serbians in Poland

1. The employer applies for a work permit (zezwolenie na pracę) at the voivode (wojewoda) for its registered seat. The rules changed on 1 June 2025, and the general labour-market test was abolished, according to commercial guides. Fees from 1 Dec 2025 are 200 PLN (up to 3 months) or 400 PLN (longer), with a pay floor at the 2026 minimum wage of 4,806 PLN gross for full-time work. Sources: https://inewi.pl/blog/zatrudnianie-cudzoziemcow-w-2025-najnowsze-zmiany-w-przepisach and https://cgomobility.pl/zatrudnienie-cudzoziemca/zezwolenie-na-prace/ . Verify on praca.gov.pl.
2. A work permit alone does not give residence. The guides point to a temporary residence and work permit (type A). The source says it is applied for where the foreigner lives, which is unclear for someone outside Poland. Check this at the consulate or the voivodeship office. The simplified declaration route (oświadczenie) is for other nationalities. My understanding is Serbia is not on that list, but this was not confirmed in the searches.
3. For international haulage, the carrier typically needs a driver attestation (świadectwo kierowcy) from GITD for non-EU drivers. It is valid up to 5 years. Sources disagree on whether it applies to vehicles under 3.5 t. The carrier applies, not the driver. Sources: https://rpms.pl/swiadectwo-kierowcy-w-transporcie-miedzynarodowym-jak-uzyskac-i-kogo-dotyczy/ and https://tckancelaria.eu/poradnik/kto-wystepuje-o-swiadectwo-kierowcy/ . This is a carrier-side step, so ask the employer whether it will file.
4. Reality check: most Polish ads are in Polish with no language or permit clause. Expect the employer to need a 3-6 month permit lead time, and check the Serbian CPC status against the Polish Code 95 requirement before applying. The candidate's Slovenian Code 95 has expired, and Polish ads often require a valid 95. Also check the candidate's C category history and the 21-year age rule for C+E.
5. Red flags to check: none of the snippets showed fees charged to drivers, "free visa" claims, USD salaries or wage reviews. This was not a full review, so run those checks on each ad before applying.
