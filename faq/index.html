Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about how we help with your Malta trip.

About the Service

What exactly do you do?
We help you plan your Malta stay from start to finish. This includes helping you choose accommodation that fits your style and budget, organizing transport from the airport, planning realistic daily itineraries, and providing real-time support via WhatsApp while you're in Malta. We're not booking agents—we're your local guides helping you make the right decisions.
Are you a travel agency?
No. We're not a traditional travel agency. We don't sell packages or own accommodations. We're a coordination and planning service that helps you find the right options, understand the trade-offs, and make confident decisions. All bookings are made directly with hotels, transfer companies, and other providers.
How quickly do you respond?
We typically respond within 1-2 hours during business hours. For time-sensitive issues while you're in Malta, we prioritize responses. WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us.
Can you help with last-minute bookings?
Yes. If you're arriving soon and need accommodation or transport arranged quickly, we can often find options within hours. The earlier you contact us, the more options we have available.
How does your service work?
We usually start with a quick conversation about your dates, budget, and preferences. For simple questions, we're happy to help directly at no cost. For more detailed planning—multiple accommodation options with comparisons, full trip setup, or structured itineraries—we offer a clear paid service. This ensures you get focused, high-quality help without endless back and forth. We discuss pricing upfront before starting any detailed work.

Accommodation Help

How do you choose accommodation for me?
We ask about your budget, preferred areas, what matters to you (sea view, walkability, quiet, nightlife proximity), and any special needs. Then we recommend 2-3 specific options we think match your preferences, explain why each works for different reasons, and help you decide.
Do you charge if I don't book?
We don't charge for quick advice or initial suggestions. However, detailed accommodation searches with multiple tailored options and analysis require our paid service. If you're looking for comprehensive help, we'll clearly explain pricing before we start work. This ensures you get quality recommendations without unnecessary back and forth.
Can you negotiate rates?
Sometimes. We have relationships with some properties that allow us to offer slight discounts or upgrades. We always work to get you the best value, but pricing depends on availability and the provider's policies.
What if I don't like a recommendation?
Tell us what didn't work and why. We'll adjust and provide different options. We keep iterating until we find something that feels right for you.

Transfers & Transport

How do you handle airport transfers?
We help you understand your options: taxi from the airport, pre-arranged transfer service, or car rental. For each option, we explain the cost, time, convenience, and what to expect. Then we help coordinate if you'd like.
Is it safe to take a taxi from the airport?
Yes, official airport taxis are reliable and regulated. We can advise on approximate costs and best practices. If you prefer a pre-arranged service for peace of mind, we can organize that too.
Do I need a car in Malta?
Not necessarily. Malta has good public transport via buses. If you're based in a central area and don't plan many remote destinations, a car isn't essential. We help you decide based on your itinerary.

Itineraries & Activities

Can you plan a full itinerary for me?
Yes. We create realistic day-by-day plans based on how you actually travel. We tell you what to see, realistic timing, where to eat, and how to move between locations. We avoid over-planning so you have flexibility.
Are your itineraries adventurous or relaxed?
We adapt. Some travelers want to see everything—we pack the days. Others prefer slower pace with downtime—we give you that space. Tell us your style and we plan accordingly.
What if I want to skip something in the itinerary?
Absolutely. An itinerary is a guide, not a rule. Skip anything that doesn't appeal and use the free time however you want. We're just here to give you good options.

Travel Assistance While You're Here

What kind of support do you offer during the trip?
Real questions, real answers. Questions about a neighborhood, changes to your plans, restaurant recommendations, how to get somewhere, whether to do something, or last-minute decisions. We're here to help you make smart choices in the moment.
Are you available 24/7?
We're available during normal business hours and respond quickly. For genuine emergencies unrelated to bookings or planning, contact local emergency services. For trip-related issues, WhatsApp us anytime and we'll help when we're available.
What if my accommodation cancels on me?
We help you quickly find alternatives and move forward. While we can't force a provider to honor a booking, we have relationships that help us find solutions faster than you could alone.

Pricing & Payments

How much does this cost?
Quick answers to simple questions are free. Detailed accommodation research, comparisons of 3+ options, full itinerary planning, or ongoing trip support are part of our paid service. The fee depends on complexity and scope. We always explain pricing upfront before starting detailed work—no surprises, no invoices after the fact.
What if I just have a quick question?
Quick questions are always free. Ask us anything about Malta, neighborhoods, transport, food, or where to go. We help because it's what we do.

Changes & Cancellations

What if I need to change my booking?
Changes depend on the provider's policy for that specific booking. We help coordinate the change with the provider. Some changes are free, others may have fees—we'll let you know upfront.
Can I cancel?
For our planning service, cancellation depends on timing and work completed. For third-party bookings (hotels, transfers), the provider's cancellation policy applies, not ours. Always review the provider's terms before booking.

Didn't find your answer?

Reach out directly via WhatsApp and we'll give you a straight answer. No lengthy forms, no waiting around.
